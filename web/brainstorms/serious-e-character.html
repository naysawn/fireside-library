How it works

Read together

Each module is a written text designed to be read aloud in a small group. The material provides structure, so the conversation can go deep without losing focus.

Explore and discuss

Discussion prompts throughout each module invite participants to share what they think and feel. The best learning happens when people explore ideas together.

Go deeper over time

Modules span three phases: discovery for newcomers, investigation for deeper study, and consolidation for committed exploration. Start anywhere.

An experiment

This started in Boston.
We need your help.

These modules began as an experiment by one community trying to make teaching conversations more sustained and meaningful. They are not polished institutional output. They are people who care deeply, trying something, and sharing what they have learned.

In practice, they have sparked three-hour discussions on deep questions of meaning, purpose, and faith. We think that is worth sharing. We need other communities to try them and tell us what works.
