Why these exist

We kept running into the same problem. Here is how we solved it.

1

Deep conversations are hard to sustain

Even with the best intentions, discussions about subjects that matter tend to lose focus or stay on the surface. The desire is there. The structure is not.

2

Written material gives conversations an anchor

Having something to read together changes everything. The text keeps the discussion grounded while questions open up new ways of understanding.

3

We learn by exploring together

Nobody processes the Faith just by reading a text. We need to ask questions, hear how others understand it, sit with ideas that challenge us. These modules create that space.

An experiment from Boston. Open source.

A small group of Baha'is built these and tested them in real sessions. They are free, open source, and far from finished. We need other communities to try them and tell us what works.
