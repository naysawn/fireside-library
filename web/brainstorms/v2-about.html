About this project

A small group of Baha'is in Boston built these modules. Here is why, and how you can help.

Why teach?

Teaching the Faith is not one obligation among many. Baha'u'llah calls it "the most meritorious of all deeds." Shoghi Effendi urged the American believers to make it "the dominating passion of our life." The Universal House of Justice describes it as "the sacred act of assisting a soul to recognize and obey his Lord."

"Teach ye the Cause of God, O people of Baha, for God hath prescribed unto every one the duty of proclaiming His Message, and regardeth it as the most meritorious of all deeds."

Baha'u'llah

The world needs the Baha'i teachings. The Universal House of Justice puts it plainly: the body of humanity's material civilization "calls aloud, yearns more desperately with each passing day, for its Soul." Only those who have themselves awakened to the Revelation of God can bring this help to others.

Why written materials?

Having something to read together changes a conversation. It keeps the focus on subjects of depth and gives everyone an anchor point. This is not a new idea.

In Colombia, during the growth that eventually led to the Ruhi materials, seekers studied from a booklet called Conozcamos La Fe Baha'i. The most rapid rise of enrollments in the United States happened when communities used the "Red Teaching Book" as material while travel teachers answered questions.

Written materials work because they give people something concrete to respond to. The text matters, but what makes these modules effective is that they get people talking and sharing. In practice, they have sparked three-hour discussions on deep questions of meaning, purpose, and faith.

How they work

Each module covers one subject in a single session of two to three hours. The format is simple.

1

Pick a module

Choose a topic based on what your group or seeker is interested in. Each module is self-contained.

2

Read together

Go through the two sections as a group. The text includes readings from the Baha'i writings alongside explanatory material.

3

Discuss

Each section has reflection questions that draw people into conversation. This is where the real learning happens.

4

Follow up

Some groups continue with another module. Others move to Ruhi Book 1. The modules are flexible enough to fit your situation.

How they are made

Every module goes through a research and testing process before it reaches version 1.

Research
Gather source texts and structure the topic
→
Draft
Write the module and discussion questions
→
Review
Edit for accuracy, tone, and flow
→
Test
Use it in real sessions and collect feedback
→
Publish
Release as v1 and keep improving

Get involved

We need communities to test these modules, give feedback, and help us learn what works. Writers, editors, and facilitators are all welcome.
