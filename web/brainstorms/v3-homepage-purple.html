Written materials for sharing the Baha'i Faith

Twelve modules on subjects that matter to seekers. Each one gives a small group something to read together, questions to sit with, and a reason to keep talking for three hours.

Browse modules About this project

How a module works

Every module covers one subject and runs in a single session of two to three hours. You can do them in any order. Each module has two sections, and each section follows the same rhythm.

01

Reflection questions

Each section opens with questions that get people thinking before they read anything. These are not icebreakers. They ask the group to consider what they actually believe about the subject at hand.

02

Reading passage with quotations

The group reads a passage together that weaves explanatory text with quotations from the Baha'i writings. The text gives context. The quotations give weight. Reading out loud makes it a shared experience.

03

Discussion questions

After the reading, discussion questions push the conversation deeper. These ask people to connect what they just read to their own lives and to each other's perspectives.

How to use them

You do not need training or facilitation experience. Pick a module, gather some people, and follow these steps.

01

Find people who are genuinely curious about the Faith. Friends, co-workers, neighbors. People who have real questions and want to explore them.

02

Print the module so everyone has a copy. People engage differently when they can hold the text, underline things, and flip back to a passage someone mentioned earlier.

03

Sit together. Groups of 4 to 8 work best, with at least 2 Baha'is in the mix. Small enough for real conversation. Large enough for different perspectives.

04

Read and discuss together. Go through the module section by section. Read the passages out loud. Let the questions do the work. The best sessions are the ones where people forget to check the time.

Still an experiment

This project started in Boston. A few of us wanted better materials for sharing the Faith with seekers, so we started writing them. We tested them in living rooms and over Zoom. Some worked well. Some we rewrote from scratch. We are still learning what works.

Everything here is open source. The modules, the writing process, and the tools we use to produce them are all on GitHub. We need other communities to try these, give us honest feedback, and help us figure out what we have not thought of yet.
