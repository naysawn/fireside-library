Baha'i.Modules A place to start exploring together

Come sit with us.

These are structured study materials for small groups exploring the Baha'i Faith together. Each module is a guided conversation — a mix of readings, reflections, and questions designed so you can explore at your own pace.

How it works

Three ingredients, zero pressure.

Gather a small group

Two friends around a kitchen table works beautifully. So does six people in a living room. There is no wrong number.

Read and reflect together

Each module weaves together Baha'i writings with guided questions. Think of it as a conversation starter with depth built in.

Go at your own pace

There is no curriculum clock. Spend two sessions on a module or five. Revisit something that resonated. Follow your curiosity.
