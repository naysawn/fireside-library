About Modules

Deep conversations about faith are hard to sustain.

Most drift after an hour. The desire is there, but keeping a group engaged on questions of real depth — for two, three hours — is genuinely difficult. Even when everyone in the room cares deeply.

Written materials change that. When a group has something to read together, the conversation finds an anchor. It stays on subjects of substance instead of wandering. People go deeper instead of wider.

These modules are materials designed for people to explore the Baha'i Faith together. Not to read alone. To read, discuss, and sit with ideas that challenge and illuminate.

How they work

01

Read together

Each module is a written text designed for a single session of two to three hours. A facilitator guides the group through the material, reading passages aloud.

02

Discuss what you've read

Discussion prompts are woven throughout. They draw participants into conversation about the ideas, connecting the teachings to their own experience and questions.

03

Explore at your own pace

Every module stands on its own. Start with whatever subject calls to you. There is no required sequence — only three phases that reflect a natural progression of interest.

About this project

An experiment,
shared openly.

This project started in Boston as an experiment in creating better materials for teaching the Baha'i Faith. It is still an experiment. We are a group of people who care deeply about sharing the Faith, trying something, and learning from what happens.

The modules, the process, and the tools are all open source. We need other communities to try these materials, tell us what works, and help us improve them. This is not polished institutional output. It is an honest effort, shared with the hope that others will find it useful.
