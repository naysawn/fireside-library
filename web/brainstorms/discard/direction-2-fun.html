Bite-sized explorations of the Baha'i Faith built for small groups and real conversations. Read together. Reflect. Discuss. No lectures, no jargon, no prerequisites.

How it works

Pick a module.
Gather some people.
Have an actual conversation.

01
Pick your module

Each one covers a different topic. Start wherever you are curious. There is no required order, no syllabus, no test at the end.

02
Read together

Two short sections. Read them aloud in a small group. The module includes original Baha'i writings alongside accessible explanations.

03
Reflect and discuss

Every section ends with questions that actually make you think. Not busywork. Real questions about real things that matter to real people.
