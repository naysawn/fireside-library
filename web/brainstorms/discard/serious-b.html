About Modules

Explore the Faith together.

Twelve written modules designed to anchor deep conversations about the Baha'i Faith—so the discussion stays focused, and the exploration stays real.

The problem

Deep conversations are hard to sustain.

Even among people who care deeply, discussions about the Faith tend to drift. After an hour or two the energy fades, the thread is lost, and the group moves on before reaching anything of real depth.

Which raised a question: what if the problem is not the people, but the format?

The insight

Written material gives a conversation its anchor.

When a group reads something together, the discussion has structure. There is a shared reference point. People can go deeper because they are not starting from scratch—the text holds the center while the conversation explores around it.

So we set out to create that anchor—material built not for reading alone, but for reading together.

What we built

Materials for exploring together.

We do not learn by reading alone. We need to sit with ideas, hear how others understand them, and let ourselves be challenged. These modules are built for exactly that—group exploration of the Baha'i Faith, at whatever depth the group is ready for.

Open Source

An experiment from Boston.

This project started when a group of Baha'is in Boston kept running into the same problem: they wanted deeper study sessions for seekers and friends, but there was not much material designed for group exploration. So they started writing their own.

It is still an experiment. The modules are not perfect. They are the best we have managed so far—written by people who care deeply, revised with honest feedback, and shared openly so other communities can try them too.

We need your help. Try a module with your community. Tell us what worked and what did not. Fork the project and adapt it. This only gets better with more people involved.
