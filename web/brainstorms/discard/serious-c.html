Three-hour conversations about God, purpose, and what it means to be human.

Deep conversations about the Baha'i Faith are hard to sustain. Even when the desire is there, most groups lose focus within the first hour. The discussion drifts, energy fades, the moment passes.

These modules change that. They give your group something to read together — a shared anchor that keeps the conversation grounded in subjects of real depth. Not lectures. Not textbooks. Materials designed for people to explore the Faith together.

Browse modules 12 modules across 3 phases

How it works

We don't learn by just reading. We need to explore together — to ask questions, hear how others understand the teachings, and sit with ideas that challenge us.

Each module is designed for a single session of two to three hours, combining readings with discussion prompts that draw participants into genuine conversation.

01

Pick a module

Choose a topic that fits your group. Each module stands on its own — no prerequisites, no sequence required.

02

Read together

The text is meant to be read aloud in a group. Sections alternate between readings and guided discussion.

03

Let the conversation happen

The prompts do the work. Groups regularly go three hours without losing focus — on questions of meaning, purpose, and faith.

An open source experiment from Boston.

This project started in a living room. A small group of Baha'is in Boston wanted better materials for teaching the Faith, so we started writing them. It is still an experiment — we are doing our best, and we need other communities to try these and tell us what works.
