Baha'i Modules

A structured curriculum for exploring the Baha'i Faith — from first encounter to deep commitment. Each module stands on its own. Together, they form a path.

How it works

01

Discovery

Foundational modules for those encountering the Baha'i Faith for the first time. Clear, welcoming, no assumptions.

02

Investigation

Deeper explorations for seekers ready to examine specific teachings, history, and how the Faith addresses the modern world.

03

Consolidation

Modules for those committed to understanding the Faith's organizational life and how its principles translate into action.
