# Character / Mascot Brainstorm

## The role of the character

The character is the visual personality of the site. It should:
- Make the site feel approachable and memorable
- Work at tiny sizes (nav icon) and larger sizes (hero)
- Have a consistent personality across contexts
- Tie directly to the accent color
- Not be silly or cartoonish, but have warmth

## Idea 1: An open book

A simple book, slightly open, with pages fanning out. Geometric, clean.

**Why it works:** The modules ARE written materials. A book is the most literal representation.

**Personality options:**
- Pages gently turning (subtle animation)
- A warm glow coming from between the pages (the knowledge within)
- Small eyes on the spine giving it a friendly face
- The accent color as the cover, white pages

**Where it appears:**
- Nav: tiny book icon, closed
- Hero: larger book, open, maybe with a glow
- Loading state: pages flipping
- Empty state: book closed, waiting

**Risk:** Books are a common icon. Could feel generic without strong personality.

## Idea 2: A candle / flame

A simple candle with a flame. The flame is the accent color.

**Why it works:** Candles represent illumination, gathering together, warmth. Study groups often feel like sitting around a shared light. The Baha'i writings frequently use light and flame as metaphors for the Word of God. Baha'u'llah wrote about prayer being "a fire that burns away the veils."

**Personality options:**
- The flame flickers gently (CSS animation)
- The flame could have a subtle face (two dot eyes)
- Multiple candles for the "gathering" concept (one for each person in a group)
- The candle could be simple and geometric (rectangle + teardrop flame)

**Where it appears:**
- Nav: single small flame (no candle body needed at this size)
- Hero: full candle with animated flame
- Section dividers: a row of small flames
- Footer: small candle

**Strength:** Deeply tied to the content's spiritual nature without being explicitly religious iconography.

## Idea 3: A page / document with a spark

A single page or sheet of paper with a small spark or star on it. The spark is the accent color.

**Why it works:** It represents the module itself. The spark suggests something alive within the text, that these are not dead documents but living materials that ignite conversation.

**Personality options:**
- The spark pulses gently
- Small face on the page (friendly document)
- The page could be slightly curled at one corner (feels tangible, not flat)
- Multiple pages slightly overlapping (suggesting the collection)

**Where it appears:**
- Nav: small page icon with spark
- Hero: larger page with animated spark
- Module cards: each card could have the page character
- Download button: the page "flies" to you

**Strength:** Unique. Nobody else uses "a page with a spark" as a mascot.

## Idea 4: A circle of dots (people gathering)

Abstract representation of people sitting in a circle. Simple dots or small figures arranged in a ring.

**Why it works:** The modules are fundamentally about gathering together. The circle of people IS the product.

**Personality options:**
- Dots gently pulse or breathe
- One dot is the accent color (you, joining the circle)
- The circle could have a warm glow in the center
- At small sizes, just 4-5 dots in a loose circle

**Where it appears:**
- Nav: tiny cluster of dots
- Hero: larger circle with gentle animation
- "How to use them" section: the dots illustrate the group
- Footer: dots

**Risk:** Abstract. Might not be immediately recognizable as a "character."

## Idea 5: A lantern (revisited from variant E)

A simple geometric lantern with a warm glow. Already prototyped.

**Why it works:** Lanterns guide, illuminate, and are carried by hand. You bring a lantern to share light with others. The lantern holds the flame within a structure, the way a module holds the teachings within a format.

**Already tested:** The lantern from serious-e had good reception. Could be refined.

## Recommendation

**Candle or lantern** feels strongest for this project. Both connect to light, gathering, and the spiritual nature of the content without being explicitly religious symbols (like a nine-pointed star would be). The candle is simpler and easier to render at small sizes. The lantern has more visual interest and uniqueness.

A candle also pairs naturally with the "how to use them" message: gather some friends, light a candle (metaphorically), and read together.
