Baha'i Modules An open source project

We don't learn about faith by reading alone. We learn by exploring together.

We kept running into the same problem. A group of friends would gather to talk about the Baha'i Faith, and the conversation would be wonderful for the first hour. Then it would drift. People would trail off into tangents, or the discussion would stay on the surface when everyone wanted to go deeper. The desire was there. Sustaining it was the hard part.

So we started writing things down. Not scripts. Not lectures. Just clear, well-structured texts that a group could read together and then discuss. Written material turned out to be the missing piece. It gave conversations an anchor—a shared reference point that kept the group focused on subjects of real substance instead of wandering.

That is what these modules are: materials designed for people to explore the Baha'i Faith together. Each one covers a single subject, runs two to three hours, and combines readings from the Baha'i writings with questions that draw people into conversation. In practice, they have sparked the kind of three-hour discussions we once struggled to sustain for one.

This started as an experiment in Boston. It is still an experiment. We are a small group of people who care about teaching the Faith, trying something, and sharing what we have so far. The modules, the process, the tools—all of it is open source. We need other communities to try these, tell us what works, and help us make them better.

Modules
Discovery

Foundational subjects for those encountering the Baha'i Faith for the first time. Clear, welcoming, no prior knowledge assumed.

  • 01 Overview of the Baha'i Faith Discovery
  • 02 Baha'i Spiritual Principles Discovery
  • 03 Baha'i Social Teachings Discovery
  • 05 The Bab and Baha'u'llah Discovery
  • 08 Prayer Discovery
Investigation

Deeper explorations for those ready to examine specific teachings, history, and how the Faith addresses the questions of our time.

  • 04 Developing a Relationship with God Investigation
  • 06 The Centers of the Covenant Investigation
  • 07 Making Sense of a Turbulent World Investigation
  • 09 The Baha'i Faith and Christianity Investigation
  • 11 Suffering and Justice Investigation
Consolidation

For those committed to understanding how the Faith's principles translate into organizational life and collective action.

  • 10 The Baha'i Administrative Order Consolidation
  • 12 Non-Involvement in Partisan Politics Consolidation

An open source project from Boston. These modules are free to use, adapt, and share. If you use them with your community, we'd love to hear how it went.
