How it works

Read together

Each module is a written text designed to be read aloud in a small group. The material gives the conversation an anchor, so it can go deep without drifting.

Explore and discuss

We don't learn by just reading. Discussion prompts throughout each module invite participants to share what they think and feel. The best learning happens when people explore ideas together.

Go deeper over time

Modules span three phases: discovery for newcomers, investigation for deeper study, and consolidation for committed exploration. Start wherever feels right.

An experiment

Started in Boston.
Still figuring it out.

These modules began as an experiment by one community trying to make teaching conversations more sustained and meaningful. This is not polished institutional output. It's an open source project by people who care deeply, doing their best, and sharing what they've learned.

We need other communities to try these and tell us what works. In practice, they've sparked three-hour discussions on questions of meaning, purpose, and faith. We think that's worth sharing.
