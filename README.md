# Baha'i Modules

Written materials for exploring the Baha'i Faith together. Bite-sized written explorations built for small groups and real conversations.

## Why these exist

Having written material to read together helps sustain conversation and keeps the focus on subjects worth discussing. Without something to look at together, conversations drift — to the weather, to sports, to anything but subjects of real meaning.

The pattern is simple: give a small group something to read together, ask questions that cause people to think, and let the conversation go where it goes. In our experience, that combination has sparked three-hour discussions about suffering, the existence of God, what happens after death, and what it means to live a spiritual life in this century.

## Who these are for

Every module targets a specific stage of a seeker's relationship with the Faith. The subjects, the depth, and the assumptions about what a reader already knows shift accordingly.

- **Discovery** — first encounter with the Faith. The goal is a genuine "oh wow" reaction.
- **Investigation** — actively exploring. Deeper engagement with harder questions.
- **Consolidation** — identifies as Baha'i and wants to understand community life, institutions, and ways to serve.
- **Service** — groups continue with Ruhi and other institute materials.

## Repository

This is a monorepo:

- `content/` — module drafts, briefs, and editorial analysis
- `web/` — Astro publishing site

This project started in Boston with a small group of Baha'is who wanted better materials for sharing the Faith with seekers. It is open source. We are still experimenting, still learning, still rewriting — if you want to try these in your community, adapt them, or help write new ones, you are welcome.
