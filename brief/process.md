# Module Development Process

## 1. Planning (Group)

- Group identifies ~10 modules for the next cycle based on the pipeline of existing seekers
- For each module, the group agrees on:
  - 2 sections, 500-750 words per section
  - Bullet points for each section
  - A timeline for completion
  - Questions from people at the grassroots
  - A paid professional writer and a volunteer editor with contacts at the grassroots in Boston

## 2. Drafting (Writer)

- Find source materials already written on the topic
- Leverage Claude to produce a draft
- Share the draft with the editor

## 3. Editorial Review (Editor)

The editor provides feedback with an eye towards:
- How is the arc of the draft?
- Is there better source material to leverage?
- What overall principles should be better emphasized?
- What should be better emphasized to address questions at the grassroots?

## 4. Rewrite (Writer)

- Writer rewrites the module in their own words
- AI may be leveraged but the finished product should feel like a human wrote the text

## 5. Beta Publication

- Material is published and labelled as beta

## 6. Field Testing

- Feedback is actively sought each time the module is used, at least 2 times
- Feedback is written down and provided back to the writer

## 7. Final Publication

- Writer incorporates feedback into a new version
- Module is published again, upgrading its status to v1

## Complementary Resources

- A library of source materials (digital e-books) will be collected on a Google Drive
- A website to publish modules will be created
